Privacy policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Who runs the program. Octopus Red Barn Vendor Checks is operated for and by Red Barn Wholesale (Shelby Township, Michigan) for its own books only. It is not a public service and has no users outside Red Barn Wholesale.

What data it handles. Vendor names, vendor mailing addresses, purchase-order numbers and amounts from Red Barn's own inventory system, and the vendor, account and check records of Red Barn's own QuickBooks Online company. It does not handle consumer data, payment-card data or data of any other company.

What it does with the data. It creates checks in Red Barn's QuickBooks Online company for the purchase orders Red Barn's staff approved, and emails the weekly list to Red Barn's own staff. Nothing is sold, rented or shared with anyone else. The only outside service it talks to is Intuit's QuickBooks Online, through Intuit's official interface.

Where the data lives. On Red Barn Wholesale's own server. The QuickBooks connection tokens are kept there too, in a file that only the program reads. They are never published or sent anywhere but to Intuit.

Disconnecting. A QuickBooks Online admin of Red Barn Wholesale can disconnect the program at any time from QuickBooks (Apps or Integrations). After that the program can no longer reach QuickBooks.
